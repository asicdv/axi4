How to use this

  1. Open the visual and the workbook side by side. Every visual has an open workbook → button that launches the matching case in a new window. Drag them beside each other.
  2. Read the workbook's background section first — why the case matters and where it shows up in real silicon.
  3. Predict before you look. Work sections 2 and 3 from the configuration alone. Getting a prediction wrong is the most useful thing that can happen.
  4. Then open the visual and check yourself. Press check in the workbook to grade the exact answers and the waveform.
  5. Self-score the written answers honestly. The review queue surfaces anything you marked missed after a week.
